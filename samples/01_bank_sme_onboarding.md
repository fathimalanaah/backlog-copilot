# FICTIONAL SAMPLE — not real client data

Workshop: SME account onboarding — Gulf Horizon Bank (fictional)
Date: 10 November 2026
Attendees: Head of SME Banking, two SME relationship managers, compliance officer, IT lead

## Notes

The Head of SME Banking opened by saying onboarding a new SME customer takes 12 working days on average and the target is 5 working days.

Relationship managers said RMs keep asking customers twice for the same trade licence because documents are emailed and saved in different folders. They want one checklist per application showing which documents are received and which are missing.

Required documents for every application: trade licence, memorandum of association, Emirates ID of each shareholder, proof of address, and the latest bank statement.

The compliance officer said no account can be opened until compliance has signed off the KYC review. Every sign-off must record who approved and when. She also said a rejected application must keep its documents for audit.

RMs asked for a reminder when a document has been missing for more than 3 working days, so they can chase the customer.

The Head of SME Banking wants a weekly dashboard showing how many applications are in each stage and the average days per stage.

The IT lead said the first release must not integrate with the core banking system; account numbers will be created manually for now. Integration is a later phase.

There was disagreement about the SLA for compliance review: one RM said compliance must respond within 2 working days, while the compliance officer said 3 working days is realistic. This was not resolved.

Decision: the first release is for internal staff only; customers will not log in.

Open point: nobody confirmed whether RMs can upload documents on behalf of customers from a mobile phone.
