# FICTIONAL SAMPLE — not real client data

Workshop: Trade permit renewal — Al Rimal Municipality Service Centre (fictional)
Date: 12 November 2026
Attendees: Service centre manager, two customer service agents, permits supervisor, finance officer

## Notes

The service centre manager said permit renewals currently arrive by email and walk-in, and agents re-type every request into a spreadsheet. About 400 renewals arrive each month.

Agents want an online renewal request form that captures the permit number, company name, contact email and the renewal period of 1 or 2 years.

The permits supervisor said a renewal cannot be approved if the company has unpaid fines. The agent must see the outstanding fine amount before sending the request for approval.

Every renewal request must get a reference number that the customer can quote when they call.

The finance officer said the renewal fee is AED 1,200 for 1 year and AED 2,200 for 2 years, and the fee must be shown before the customer submits.

Agents asked for a search by permit number or reference number so they can answer phone calls quickly.

The service centre manager wants requests older than 5 working days without a decision to be highlighted for the supervisor.

Decision: renewals for permits that expired more than 6 months ago are out of scope; those go through the new-permit process.

Open point: the team did not agree whether customers should receive an SMS or only an email when the renewal is approved.
